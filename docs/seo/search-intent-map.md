## Page-to-intent contract

### `/`

- Primary job: understand whether an existing commercial awning can receive a custom illuminated
  replacement valance and submit the object for review.
- Must answer: exact product, what remains/changes, compatibility, measurements/Keder, artwork
  limits, cable/power/electrical responsibility, local permission boundary, complete scope/cost
  drivers, real evidence and next step.
- Primary CTA: `Projekt prüfen lassen`.
- May satisfy exact category, qualified retrofit, initial gastronomy and cost intent on one useful
  canonical page.
- Must not pretend to answer new-awning, ambient lighting, repair, DIY, replacement-part or B2C
  terrace intent.
- By owner decision, the homepage owns this intent; do not create the formerly proposed
  `/beleuchteter-markisen-volant` duplicate.

### `/konfigurator`

- Job: configure one illuminated-valance composition for a commercial project, receive the current
  server-reproduced preliminary net component result and submit the same shared project inquiry.
- Must provide useful server-rendered explanation, unique metadata/H1, clean self-canonical,
  visible calculation limitations and manual-review services.
- Must not target consumer total-price intent, imply compatibility/final quotation or publish state
  variants as URLs. `Product`/`Offer` Schema remains absent.

### `/referenzen`

- Job: evaluate real completed work and build trust.
- Requires substantive, permission-cleared projects with descriptive text and useful imagery.
- Do not launch as an empty gallery or stock-photo collection.

### `/kontakt`

- Job: reach the real provider or submit an inquiry.
- Not a substitute for service content.

### Legal routes

`/impressum` and `/datenschutz` serve trust/compliance, not keyword acquisition. They must remain
accurate and accessible without being optimized as campaign landing pages.

## Candidate future routes

Create only after query evidence, business fit and unique content are approved:

- gastronomy solution with a genuine hospitality case;
- hotel and retail solutions with their own buyer/process evidence;
- planning/installation process if it answers a distinct user job;
- partner/supplier route after contractual programme approval;
- verified regional service pages;
- useful price/cost guide after actual German quotes;
- references after substantive, permission-cleared projects.

Every new route needs:

1. distinct user job;
2. real service/product capability;
3. unique evidence and copy;
4. approved canonical/internal-link placement;
5. matching Ads destination if used;
6. a measurement and maintenance owner.

## Likely exclusions for research

High-confidence non-target/noise groups:

- camper/vehicle: `Wohnmobil`, `Wohnwagen`, `Camper`, `Fiamma`, `Thule`, `Dometic`, `Omnistor`;
- ambient/new system: `LED Streifen`, `Lichtleiste`, `Lichterkette`, `Spot`, `Gelenkarm`,
  `Kassette`, `Volant Plus`, `Vario-Volant`, `Sichtschutz`, `Klemmmarkise`, `neue Markise`;
- repair/DIY/shop: `Ersatzstoff`, `Ersatztuch`, `reparieren`, `nähen`, `selber machen`, `DIY`,
  `Anleitung`, `gebraucht`, marketplaces and price-comparison shopping.

Do not broadly exclude `nachrüsten`, `austauschen`, `bestehend`, `Logo`, `Volant`, `Gastronomie`,
`nach Maß`, `Preis`, `Kosten`, `bedrucken` or `Beschriftung`; qualified combinations can match the
product. This remains a seed set, not a deployed campaign list.

## Research method

Before final copy, routes or campaigns:

Completed:

1. narrowed the product and primary B2B segment;
2. expanded exact, retrofit, vertical, cost, partner and noise variants;
3. classified a dated public German result sample;
4. grouped the viable launch intent into one canonical pillar.

Still required:

1. owner acceptance of product and geography;
2. Google Ads Keyword Planner ranges, only after permission;
3. buyer/partner vocabulary from interviews and quote calls;
4. Search Console and Ads search-term evidence after a compliant launch;
5. rejection of every route that would be thin, duplicate or outside actual service.

Record date, market (`Germany`), language (`German`), device assumptions and source for any volume
or competition number. Do not copy numbers into evergreen copy without a review date.
