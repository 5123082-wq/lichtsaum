# Content Strategy

Status: `Proposed` pending owner acceptance and product validation  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

## Purpose

Создать оригинальный немецкий контент, который одновременно:

- точно объясняет продукт;
- отвечает намерению пользователя из Search/Ads;
- подтверждает премиальное позиционирование реальными evidence;
- ведёт к одному понятному действию;
- не создаёт юридически рискованных обещаний.

## Product and category boundary

Предлагаемая категория:

`Beleuchteter Markisen-Volant für bestehende Gewerbemarkisen` — светящийся волан для уже
существующих коммерческих маркиз.

Продукт — B2B retrofit: заменяется отделяемый текстильный волан, а не вся маркиза. Рабочий scope,
флагман, ограничения и validation gates определены в
`../strategy/product-market-decision.md`.

Вспомогательные естественные варианты:

- `LED-Markisenvolant`;
- `Leuchtvolant für Markisen`;
- `beleuchteter Werbevolant`;
- `Markisenvolant mit beleuchtetem Logo`.

Не использовать как основную категорию `LED Markise`, `Markise mit Licht`, `Leuchtmarkise` или
`beleuchtete Markise`: текущая немецкая выдача преимущественно понимает их как новую B2C-маркизу,
ambient-подсветку или аксессуар для camper.

До owner acceptance и доказательства реального scope формулировка остаётся `Proposed`, а не
публичным claim.

## Audiences

| Audience | Need | Required evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent restaurants/cafés | Existing frontage remains legible and controlled after dusk without replacing the awning | Day/night case, compatibility, complete scope and price | **Primary proposed** |
| Selected bars | Strong evening need | Same proof plus price fit | Secondary |
| Independent/boutique hotels | Controlled entrance branding | Hotel-specific case, approvals and service | Secondary after proof |
| Design-led shops/boutiques | Illuminated branding on an existing awning | Retail case and comparison with unlit print | Secondary after proof |
| Chains/multi-site operators | Repeatable CI rollout | Standard pack, coverage, service and procurement evidence | Deferred |
| Awning/sign partners | A product they can specify, measure or install safely | Contracted responsibility, technical pack and margin | Channel audience |

B2C homeowners, new-awning shoppers and general outdoor-lighting buyers are outside the launch
scope. Proposed pilot geography is Berlin/Brandenburg only if the owner confirms operations; it is
not yet an allowed public claim.

## Message hierarchy

1. Exact category — an illuminated replacement valance for an existing commercial awning.
2. Outcome — `Aus Ihrer bestehenden Markise wird Markenlicht.`
3. Boundary — the existing awning remains only when compatibility is verified.
4. Difference — managed compatibility, design and delivery rather than a loose LED panel.
5. Evidence — owned day/night project, exact technical limits and named responsibility.
6. Risk reduction — object-specific review before production; no universal fit or permission claim.
7. CTA — `Projekt prüfen lassen`.

Supporting explanation must immediately make the metaphorical `Markenlicht` promise concrete.
`Kostenlos` (бесплатно) and `unverbindlich` (без обязательства) may only be used when the real
commercial process supports them.

## Landing-page content order

1. Hero with exact category, existing-awning boundary and `Projekt prüfen lassen`.
2. Rights-cleared day/night transformation.
3. Combined compatibility section: what remains, what changes, what is checked and the three
   required initial inputs.
5. Flagship outcome and engineered variants.
6. Project process and responsibility.
7. Scope and honest cost drivers.
8. Technical/electrical and object-specific permission boundary.
9. Real project evidence.
10. Comparison with unlit print, a separate sign and a new awning.
11. Product-specific FAQ.
12. Minimal project-check form and final CTA.

The detailed page contract and calculator gate are in
`../strategy/go-to-market-and-landing-brief.md`.

## Voice

- German-first, professional and direct.
- Premium through precision, not empty luxury adjectives.
- Short sentences around CTA and forms.
- Technical sections may use exact specifications after verification.
- Avoid translated Russian syntax and inflated superlatives.
- Important German public wording receives a Russian explanation in internal planning.

## Content rules

- One clear H1 per page.
- Heading hierarchy reflects the information structure.
- Each indexable page has unique useful content and a distinct search intent.
- No keyword stuffing.
- No generic city-page templates without real local value/evidence.
- No invisible SEO text, misleading hidden content or copy written only for crawlers.
- Ads promise, visible page copy and claims register must match.
- FAQ answers must be product-specific and approved; do not write invented policies.

## CTA policy

Primary:

- `Projekt prüfen lassen` (отправить проект на проверку).

Secondary:

- `So funktioniert der Retrofit` (как работает retrofit), as a process anchor;
- `Projektbeispiel ansehen` only when a real case exists.

Do not rotate primary wording across sections without a measurement reason.

## Content inputs required

- Owner acceptance of the product/segment decision.
- Supplier contract and exact supported product/options.
- Real service region and installation capacity.
- Ownership of design, measurement, removal, installation, electrical work and authority support.
- Complete-system technical ranges, ratings and incompatibility rules.
- Warranty, maintenance, replacement and service terms.
- Approved pricing/VAT policy and minimum complete order.
- Physical sample plus real day/night project evidence and publication rights.
- Legal entity, company history, team and public contact facts.
- Reviews only after authenticity and permission review.

All objective public statements must pass
`claims-and-evidence-register.md`.
